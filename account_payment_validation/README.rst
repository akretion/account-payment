This module adds a two-step approval of the payment of the vendor bills
by third-party approvers (users who are not accountants). The approval
of the vendor bill itself is done as usual: what is approved here is the
payment, the fact that the bill is ready to be paid.

- at the validation of a vendor bill, an approval request is sent by
  e-mail to the level 1 approver chosen on the bill; the request is also
  logged in the chatter of the bill;
- the level 1 approvers (members of the group *Payment Approval: Level 1
  Approvers*) can approve the first step with a dedicated button on the
  bill: the approval is then requested to the level 2 approver;
- the level 2 approvers (members of the group *Payment Approval: Level 2
  Approvers*) can approve the second step with a dedicated button: the
  bill is then approved for payment and can be paid.

The level 2 approver is mandatory to validate a vendor bill, the level 1
approver is optional: when it is not set, the approval is directly
requested to the level 2 approver.

The approval step is stored on the bill. It is displayed on the bill and
in the list views of the invoices and of the journal items, and in the
*Status* column of the invoices (the field *Status in Payment*).

The approval request can be sent again at any time with the button
*Re-send Approval Request*, which sends the request of the current step.

The approval doesn't block anything: it is a control done after the
validation of the vendor bill, before paying it.

Configuration
=============

To configure this module, you need to:

- add the users who must approve the payment of the vendor bills to the
  group *Payment Approval: Level 1 Approvers* and/or *Payment Approval:
  Level 2 Approvers* (menu *Settings > Users & Companies > Users*, the
  groups are in the *Accounting* section),
- set the *Default Level 1 Approver* and/or the *Default Level 2
  Approver* on the vendor form (tab *Invoicing*, section *Payment
  Approval*): they are then used on the vendor bills of that vendor,
  where they can still be changed bill by bill,
- check the recipients of the mail templates *Payment Approval: Level 1
  Request* and *Payment Approval: Level 2 Request* (menu *Settings >
  Technical > E-mail > Templates*): they default to the approver chosen
  on the bill, through their *Partner To* field.

The e-mails are queued by Odoo: they are sent by the scheduled action
*Mail: Email Queue Manager*.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

1. on a draft vendor bill, select the *Level 1 Approver* (optional) and
   the *Level 2 Approver* (mandatory): they are proposed from the vendor
   of the bill, and can be changed on the bill if needed,
2. validate the bill: an approval request is sent by e-mail to the level
   1 approver (or to the level 2 approver when no level 1 approver is
   set),
3. as a level 1 approver, open the bill and click on *Approve Payment
   (Level 1)*: the approval is then requested to the level 2 approver,
4. as a level 2 approver, open the bill and click on *Approve Payment
   (Level 2)*: the bill is then approved for payment and can be paid.

The approvers can open the bill from the link of the e-mail. The
approval step is displayed on the bill and in the list views of the
invoices and of the journal items. The button *Re-send Approval Request*
sends the request of the current step again, for example when the
approver didn't receive the e-mail.
