To use this module, you need to:

1. on a draft vendor bill, select the *Level 1 Approver* (optional) and the
   *Level 2 Approver* (mandatory): they are proposed from the vendor of the
   bill, and can be changed on the bill if needed,
2. validate the bill: an approval request is sent by e-mail to the level 1
   approver (or to the level 2 approver when no level 1 approver is set),
3. as a level 1 approver, open the bill and click on *Approve Payment (Level
   1)*: the approval is then requested to the level 2 approver,
4. as a level 2 approver, open the bill and click on *Approve Payment (Level
   2)*: the bill is then approved for payment and can be paid.

The approvers can open the bill from the link of the e-mail. The approval step
is displayed on the bill and in the list views of the invoices and of the
journal items. The button *Re-send Approval Request* sends the request of the
current step again, for example when the approver didn't receive the e-mail.
